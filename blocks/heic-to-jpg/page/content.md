## About this tool

iPhones and iPads save photos as **HEIC** (High Efficiency Image Container, a
HEIF file holding HEVC-encoded images) to save space. Many apps, websites, and
older devices can't open `.heic` files — they expect **JPEG** or **PNG**.

This tool converts an Apple HEIC/HEIF photo to JPEG (the default) or PNG. Pick a
`.heic` file, optionally choose `png`, and the conversion runs **entirely in your
browser** — the photo is never uploaded to a server.

- **JPEG** keeps the file small with near-lossless quality — the right choice for
  sharing photos or uploading to sites that reject HEIC.
- **PNG** is lossless and supports transparency — larger files, pixel-perfect.

Your photo stays on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere.
